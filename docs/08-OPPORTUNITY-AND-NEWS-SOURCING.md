# 08 · Opportunity and news sourcing

The current contract is [Explore simplification](15-EXPLORE-SIMPLIFICATION.md); [the original Scout rebuild](14-OPPORTUNITY-SCOUT.md) records earlier implementation history. [DEPLOY-REPLIT.md](../DEPLOY-REPLIT.md) contains publishing and owner diagnostics. News remains separate.

## Catalog-first research

1. An explicit student Search checks SQL's unexpired relevant links. Six links can cover the total target despite unknown prices, and each selected interest must be represented. Reuse costs no paid work even with AI off/capped. Interested and dismissed choices stay student-specific. Explore displays at most five Free and five Paid or cost-unconfirmed options, ranked by fit; remaining links stay stored for refill and shared reuse. This display cap does not change the existing search targets or paid limits.
2. Missing coverage queues public research. Defaults: two funded batches, at most five web tools per batch; coalescing up to three focus interests covers five selected topics without dropping the fifth. Research receives public interests/grade band/broad area/format/date. Names, schools, schedules, sign-in data and private goals/projects/time/budget never enter research or vectors.
3. Research returns named HTTPS links and optional reported details with short verbatim source quotes, at most 25 words each. Local parsing preserves valid links when optional details are malformed. Actual citations/literal response URLs can recover unreadable/truncated research; no URLs are invented. One format-only repair per whole request has no web tool. Automatic provider retries remain off.
4. Safe links are saved to SQL before enrichment. In default SCOUT_SOURCE_MODE=research_evidence, try one best-effort program-page check, then match research quotes deterministically against fetched text. No extraction AI call is made. Failed/blocked/PDF/JavaScript-only sources keep an unconfirmed card and reported-details warning. Unsupported costs/dates/eligibility remain unknown structured facts. A citation alone is not verification.
5. Source access retains public-DNS pinning, HTTPS/credential/port checks, robots rules, redirect/size/decompression/time limits and a truthful Pathway bot identity/contact URL. Robots failure remains fail-closed for automated reading; the link still displays. Cross-domain redirects require an explicit owner-reviewed domain (DISCOVERY_TRUSTED_DOMAINS or SCOUT_REDIRECT_DOMAINS), with destination DNS/robots checks. A .org suffix never establishes official status. All HTTP attempts, including robots, are recorded against the bound.
6. For an actual program link, only a confirmed past deadline hides a result. Known Maps/search listings are not program sources and cannot satisfy coverage or enter recommendations; explicit safe redirect targets preserve real links. Missing year/deadline, closed applications, grade/region/format mismatch, source age/review, fees or unfamiliar interests become warnings/ranking factors. Never infer a year or age. Common literal dates/status phrasing are accepted deterministically. Verified requires actual supported identity/audience/current details on a reviewed page; uncertainty remains Needs checking. Checks over 30 days old display as stale. Free requires source-confirmed no mandatory participation fees; other costs remain Paid or cost-unconfirmed.
7. Optional fetch_and_extract mode keeps source extraction, with roughly 12,000 relevant characters preserving original passage IDs and a maximum 90-second extraction timeout. Research can take up to 150 seconds; page stages retain their bounded deadline. Persist claims before paid calls and never replay unknown outcomes. Existing in-flight jobs retain their original source mode. New Scout source rechecks also use stored research assertions and literal page checks in evidence-first mode.
8. SQL owns public links, reports, snapshots, usage, membership and jobs. Pinecone is optional retrieval infrastructure in namespace opportunities; index failures do not remove cards. Picked/saved programs prepare actual fetched public text once per source/model/index generation. No synthetic source page is created from AI quotations. Unreadable pages can therefore leave Pinecone empty while links display correctly.
9. Ranking/preferences are local; Interested/Not for me refill without AI. Program questions use current picks plus Interested, checked every turn, with exact excerpt citations and truthful unknowns. Reminder/requirements/plan changes remain approval proposals. Nothing submits an application or edits an external calendar automatically.

## Allowances, retries and estimated spending

DISCOVERY_MONTHLY_BUDGET counts research-batch attempts, default 10/month site-wide; existing configured values are preserved. AI_MONTHLY_LIMIT counts overall accepted requests/reservations including shared jobs, while AI_DAILY_LIMIT counts interactive requests per student. These are not dollar or individual tool-call counts. Paid failures remain counted. UTC-month resets differ from personal rolling 30-day cycles.

Only searches with usable results consume the student's successful manual-search slot. Failed/empty searches preserve it; earlier empty-result charges are restored once with an audit event, without erasing paid counters. Explicit failed/empty group retries wait one hour, with at most three unsuccessful attempts per group per UTC day. No timer automatically starts a paid retry. Shared library reuse remains available during cooldowns and caps.

DISCOVERY_MONTHLY_SPEND_USD defaults to 25 for discovery estimates. Before a run starts, a serializable transaction reserves a conservative estimate in anonymous existing AIUsage keys, alongside the run/AI counters. This includes potential repair/extraction work. Pending reservations prevent concurrent runs from spending the same estimate. Completion reconciles once using observed tokens/web calls, retaining conservative estimates for unknown paid outcomes. Historical coarse estimated spending initializes the ledger; counters/history are not reset. Rechecks, Buddy, embedding and hosting costs have their separate count caps and are not a provider-wide dollar limit.

Pricing settings are estimates: SCOUT_WEB_SEARCH_PRICE_USD=0.025, SCOUT_INPUT_PRICE_PER_MILLION=4, SCOUT_OUTPUT_PRICE_PER_MILLION=16. They are not current vendor prices. At two batches and five web tools each, the tool component is at most $0.25 at that configured rate, plus tokens and any format repair. Default evidence-first reservation is approximately $0.5506/run, rounded upward; unused estimated funds release after settlement. Input usage can exceed the assumed reserve, so this is not a hard provider billing guarantee. Set provider-side spending controls/alerts too and check actual invoices.

## Diagnostics and owner test

Signed-in GET /api/discovery/status summarizes only the student's request: links, page checks, confirmations, controlled failure reasons, observed tool calls/tokens and estimates. Missing historical usage remains null. GET /internal/diagnostics adds public names/URLs/reports/source text/outcomes/configuration/month spending. JOBS_SECRET must be at least 32 characters and is sent in the Authorization Bearer header, never a URL/browser setting. Reads make no provider call. The owner live test queues one public candidate, one research request (up to five web tools), a page check and zero extraction calls in evidence-first mode. Configuration/fixture modes are synthetic and free. See the exact Shell commands in the deployment guide.

Knowledge-specific owner status and bounded explicit retry are documented in [Explore recovery](21-EXPLORE-RECOVERY.md). These checks include index/SQL readiness, real namespace counts and new attempted/acknowledged/committed counters. Read-only status sends no embedding/search request. Standalone preparation still shares `DISCOVERY_RECHECKS_PER_MONTH` and `AI_MONTHLY_LIMIT`; no quota is silently raised or usage cleared.

## News

News uses named-source links; models do not invent headlines or article text. Student-selected topics control the brief; sample news appears only with DEMO_MODE. Refresh news explicitly ingests fixed NASA, BBC News/BBC Sport and ScienceDaily RSS/Atom feeds through `data/news-feeds.ts`. It sends no student data, keeps publisher-provided metadata/excerpts, requires a real HTTPS article URL and recent publication date, and deduplicates stable URL hashes. GET only reads stored stories; Next story browses those results. A shared durable 15-minute cache and one-minute failure backoff bound fetches; 10-second deadlines and 1 MiB/30-entry caps bound each source. Private DNS/unsafe redirects and entity-bearing XML are rejected. Failed/partial feeds preserve previous real stories and show actual status. No new AI key, AI usage, schema migration or background polling is introduced; live publisher availability must be checked from the deployed environment.
