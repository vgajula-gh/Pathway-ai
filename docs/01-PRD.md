# 01 · Product Requirements — Pathway AI

**Version** 1.0 (build kit) · derived from the Pathway AI Comprehensive PDD v1.2 (Oct 10, 2026). Proposed decisions are marked *(proposed)*.

## Thesis
Make the most of today. Build a better tomorrow. Pathway AI is a personal AI companion that helps high school students simplify everyday responsibilities, discover opportunities aligned with their interests, develop valuable skills, and make meaningful progress toward their future. It is **not positioned as a planner**.

## Problem
Students balance academics, extracurriculars, competitions, volunteering, interests, skill-building and college/career prep. Information is scattered across school sites, email, calendars, social channels and program portals. The gap is not information; it is **personalized guidance, coordination and follow-through** that turns information into action.

## Who it's for
- **Primary:** students in grades 9–12.
- **Secondary:** parents, and potentially counselors, with role-based visibility, consent and limited permissions *(Phase 3+)*.

## Pillars
| Pillar | Outcome | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Discover | Find relevant, eligible opportunities | Hackathons, volunteering, internships, scholarships, competitions |
| Simplify | Reduce cognitive and logistical load | Priorities, commitments and deadlines without overscheduling |
| Grow | Develop skills and meaningful experiences | Realistic next steps, growth tracking and reflection |
| Prepare | Link today to future possibilities | Show how projects and activities build readiness |

## How the companion evolves across high school *(proposed narrative)*
| Grade | Student focus | What Pathway emphasizes |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Settle in, build habits, explore | Simple days, interest discovery, low-stakes first opportunities |
| 10 | Find what clicks | Skill paths, first competitions/clubs, reflection habit |
| 11 | Go deeper, lead | Bigger projects, leadership, readiness gaps, portfolio depth |
| 12 | Prepare and tell the story | Deadlines, scholarships, portfolio export, transitions |

## Experience principles
Student-first · Low-friction · Explainable ("why this fits") · Capacity-aware (protect rest; never fill every free hour) · Action with consent · Trustworthy (verified vs suggested, with source, date and uncertainty).

## Capabilities (MVP scope in **bold**)
- **Student DNA** — evolving profile with provenance and freshness; skip/edit/delete; no sensitive inferences.
- **News for you** — one sourced story on Today plus a feed filtered by topics the student chooses (Space, Science, AI & Tech, Health & Medicine, Climate & Nature, Arts & Culture, Sports). Defaults from interests; never generated by AI.
- **My Day** — calm daily snapshot: rings (Focus, Free time, Progress), one next best step (Do it / Later / Why this?), timeline with source tags, coming-up deadlines, optional word of the day. News only with a verified source.
- **AI Buddy** — a companion home (breathing orb, starters, "pick up where you left off", earlier chats) and separate conversation threads; new topics start new threads; conversational layer with tools; screenshot/text schedule extraction with confirmation preview; clarifying questions.
- **Opportunity Scout** — links-first discovery, transparent fit and source-backed facts. Only confirmed past deadlines hide results; eligibility, location, cost, format, application status and source confidence remain warnings/ranking factors. Primary 3+3 picks do not limit the full visible list. Verified and Needs checking are distinct. Samples require DEMO_MODE=true. See [current contract](15-EXPLORE-SIMPLIFICATION.md).
- **Action Agent** — propose → approve → execute → verify → audit; retry/manual fallback.
- **Growth Journey** — goals → small steps with effort estimates; progress narrative (not a competitive score).
- **Achievement Portfolio** — experiences, reflections, skills, visibility (private/shared), export.
- Learning Companion — explain, example, practice; integrity-safe *(basic in MVP)*.
- Exploratory (not MVP): Opportunity Gap Finder, Opportunity Creator, Adaptive Growth Coach.

## Core journeys
- **A — Discover & act:** onboard → AI interest → relevant competition → eligibility + time fit → save → approve reminder → track prep → log participation.
- **B — Simplify the week:** upload timetable → review extracted events → confirm → My Day → ask for exam support → approve schedule adjustment → mark progress.
- **C — Growth story:** volunteer → reflection → skills identified → portfolio updated → related experiences.

## Acceptance criteria (non-negotiable)
1. Onboarding works without calendar/email connections.
2. No opportunity shown as verified unless source and check time exist.
3. Ineligible or expired opportunities are excluded or prominently flagged.
4. External actions never run without a clear, specific approval.
5. Every tool action reports the actual outcome; no premature success.
6. Students can inspect, correct, export and delete profile and portfolio data.
7. Recommendations include plain-language rationale and realistic time expectations.
8. Works on common phone widths; keyboard navigation and accessible labels.
9. Missing integrations, unavailable sources and partial extraction never fail silently.

## Success measures
Onboarding completion · time-to-first-useful-recommendation · relevance feedback · saved→applied conversion · follow-through on approved actions · weekly retained use · student-reported stress reduction and agency.
**Guardrails:** false eligibility claims · stale links · excessive notifications · unauthorized actions · privacy incidents.

## Open decisions
Headline promise (growth vs everyday support) · final name (Pathway vs Pathwise) · age coverage & consent by jurisdiction · MVP sources/integrations · what the demo executes vs simulates · parent/counselor boundaries · pricing & distribution.
